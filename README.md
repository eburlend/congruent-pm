## 1. Nature and scope of the assignment

Acting as a consulting group, our team is advising **Congruent Software** (a custom software and IT consulting company based in Bellevue, WA, with a delivery center in Chennai, India) on how to improve its project management. We deliver a 15-minute presentation to Congruent's stakeholders that recommends one project management model.

**In scope**

- Congruent's essential business activities
- Six models compared: Waterfall, Agile, Waterfall-Agile hybrid, Scrum, Kanban and Lean Development
- For each model: its approach, pros and cons, tools gained, continuous improvement, and balance across developer, infrastructure and operations roles
- Each model's relation to the software development life cycle (SDLC)
- Cost analysis and benefits of each model
- A final recommendation (our recommendation: a Waterfall-Agile hybrid with Kanban for support work)
- The resources our consulting group needs to put the model in place
- Visuals throughout and APA-formatted references

**Out of scope:** building software, and contacting Congruent directly. All company information comes from public sources.

**How we work:** our team follows an Agile approach. Each task is a card on our GitHub Projects board with an owner, a due date and a status (To Do → In Progress → Done). This repo holds our documentation.
